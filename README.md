# Restoring-Wetlands-Nature-s-Water-Purifiers

Wetlands act as natural kidneys, filtering pollutants and nutrients from runoff before they reach larger water bodies. They also absorb floodwaters and provide critical habitat for migratory birds and amphibians. Restoration projects worldwide are reconnecting drained wetlands to revive these vital ecosystems. Protecting existing wetlands is often more cost-effective than building artificial treatment systems 💛💚💙
